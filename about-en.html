Our Story

From a factory floor in China to homes across Saudi Arabia — Mojin exists to make professional protection accessible to everyone.

From Factory to You

Mojin was born at 莲墨瑾服装有限公司 (Lotojin Textile Co., Ltd.), a factory with years of experience producing protective apparel. Our founder saw a problem: most PPE on the market was either overpriced or unreliable. So we decided to build our own brand — one that delivers factory-direct quality at fair prices.

Today, we export to Saudi Arabia and the Middle East, serving hospitals, clinics, businesses, and families. Every product is CE certified and tested for quality.

Our promise: Professional-grade protection, no middleman markup.
